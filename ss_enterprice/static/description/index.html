SS Enterprise

A sidebar, a chatter toggle and a login page you can brand

A light UI layer for the Odoo 20 backend. It does not replace Odoo's own design — the navbar, the views and the login card keep their standard markup and styling. Everything here is added next to them, and every part can be switched off.

Left sidebar

The menus of the app you are in, and a way to reach the others

  • Your logo on top — falls back to the company logo.
  • Pinned menus. Pin any entry; it gets its own section at the top. Pins are per user and follow them to any browser.
  • Resizable. Drag the right edge (180–480 px); double-click it to reset.
  • Collapses to an icon rail. Hover an icon and that app's menus open in a flyout — navigate without expanding again.
  • Menu groups start closed and open on click.
  • Hidden below 1024 px, where Odoo's burger menu takes over.

Chatter toggle

Give the form the full width when you do not need the chatter

  • One button in the control panel of every form that has a chatter.
  • The sheet reclaims the space — useful on wide records such as sales orders and invoices.
  • The choice is remembered per user.
  • An attachment preview keeps working while the chatter is hidden.

Login page

Your picture, your words, your layout

  • A full-screen background picture, with an adjustable dark overlay so the form stays readable on any image.
  • The card sits left, centre or right.
  • A tagline inside the card and a note underneath — a welcome line, or a support address.
  • An option to hide the "Manage Databases" link.

Everything in one place

Settings → SS Enterprise, per company

Good to know

Compatibility

  • Odoo 20.0, Community and Enterprise.
  • Depends on web and mail only.
  • Follows the light and dark colour schemes: the panel is built from Odoo's own theme variables.
